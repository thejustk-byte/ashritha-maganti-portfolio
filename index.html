02 / A little about me

Good design.
Good people.
Good energy.

I’m Ashritha, a graphic designer based in Bengaluru. My background includes fashion, and I love translating a strong idea into a visual language with personality — thoughtful in the details, clear in its message, and never afraid of a little color.

Whether I’m exploring campaign graphics, social creative or expressive typography, I’m happiest when a design feels considered and full of life.
